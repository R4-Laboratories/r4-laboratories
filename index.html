Foundational Research

Architecting Beyond Brute Force

Current deep learning scales quadratic matrix multiplication at immense energy expense. R4 Laboratories reconstructs artificial intelligence through algebraic topology, exact coordinate spaces, and discrete geometry.

Geometric Language Models (GLMs)

Autoregressive geometric state models that route through structured algebraic manifolds instead of dense quadratic self-attention layers. Token representations evolve as topological trajectories over fixed zeta and prime-address lattices.

Exact Addressed Memory & UOR

Universal Object Reference (UOR) addressing treats knowledge as invariant geometric coordinates. Eradicating probabilistic hallucination and attention decay by anchoring historical facts into addressed memory structures with verifiable provenance.

Paired Icosian & 4D Discrete Lattices

Utilizing the rich algebraic geometry of golden ratios φ, Hopf fibrations S³ → S², and 4-dimensional symmetry groups (H4 ⊕ φH4). Compute is mapped onto exact ring arithmetic and precomputed table lookups.

Autonomous Research Labs

Decentralized AI research nodes operating across multi-agent consensus protocols. Continuous peer-to-peer verification, automated hypothesis generation, and bitwise reproducible execution ledgers.
